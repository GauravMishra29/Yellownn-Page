Lucknow Residency & Martiniere School

One of the more poignant places we visited in India was the Residency in Lucknow. In 1857, this was the scene of the Seige of Lucknow,
which I remembered reading about in history lesssons at school. Here, some 2000 people - soldiers, men, women and children, as well as Indian troops
loyal to the British, died from wounds or illness or starvation during the 87 days that the seige lasted. In the Indian version of the story, this became known as "the first battle for independence" - either way, it was a miserble chapter in British history.
